Risks we planned for, and why a copy does not win

Risks

  • Masters will not join. No extra time, his name on every passport, a royalty.
  • A wrong nuance. Meanings are measured per master, never imposed.
  • Privacy. Written consent; the master owns and can delete his dictionary; data stays on the employer’s laptop.
  • Speech fails in noise. Word buttons, phone taps and transcript mining work without it.
  • Safety steps. KOTSU coaches feel only; the master signs off.

Why a copy does not win

  • The data is the product. Code copies in a month; a master’s grounded words do not.
  • Royalties keep masters. A paid master has no reason to move.
  • Records build up at every employer and academy.
  • Partner, not rival: the word layer on top of NEC’s or Mazda’s motion data.
